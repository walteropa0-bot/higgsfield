TITLE
How fast does your money double? (Rule of 72) ⏳

DESCRIPTION
Divide 72 by the interest rate = years until your money doubles.
1% savings → 72 years. ~7% stock market (long-term assumption) → ~10 years. 24% credit card debt → about 3 years (if you don't pay it down) 😬
Make 72 work FOR you, not against you.

Rule of thumb, example rates, returns are not guaranteed.
