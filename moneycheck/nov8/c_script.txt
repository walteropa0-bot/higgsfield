You put a thousand dollars into one single stock.
Best case? It's the next superstar, and you get rich.
But how likely is that?
A study tracked every US stock for one hundred years. Twenty-nine thousand companies.
Just forty-six of them created half of all the wealth.
Your odds of picking one of those: about one in six hundred thirty.
Any of the big winners at all? About one in twenty-seven.
The most likely outcome? More than half of all stocks lost money.
Meanwhile, the market as a whole turned one dollar into more than fifteen thousand.
Luck can make you rich. The odds just aren't on your side.
