Meet Jake and Emma. Same job. Same paycheck.
Jake spends it all. A new phone. New shoes. Whatever is left just sits there.
Emma works the same job. But every month, before she spends a dime, she invests three hundred dollars.
Year one? It barely shows.
Year ten: almost fifty-two thousand dollars.
Year thirty: about three hundred sixty-six thousand. Jake has nothing to show for it.
Same job. Same paycheck. The difference was never the money. It was the habit.
